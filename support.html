Support

FIT builds a timed workout for the time and gear you've got, for one or two people, then runs the clock and calls out what's next.

Getting started

The first time, FIT asks who's training and what gear you have. After that, the home screen has today's workout ready: pick how long, tap Start. To change the gear, the style or what you'd rather leave out, tap Change.

I can't hear the beeps

The sound settings are at the foot of the workout screen. On an iPhone, Beeps over my music plays alongside Spotify or other music but needs the ringer on: with the silent switch on, you won't hear FIT. Beeps even on silent plays through the silent switch, but pauses your music while you train. Check the volume too, and that No sound isn't picked.

The voice coach (it says what's next) uses your device's own voice, so it follows the volume and language set on the device. The Android app doesn't have the voice coach yet; its beeps work as usual.

Will the screen stay on?

Yes. From Start until the workout ends, pauses included, FIT keeps the screen awake, then lets it sleep again as normal. On the website that needs a browser that allows it, which current Safari and Chrome do; the app always can.

Moving to the app, or another device

The app, the website and each device keep their own data. To move yours, open Saved & ready-made where your data is now and tap Copy to another device (or Save a backup for a file). Then, on the new one, open Saved & ready-made, tap Restore, and paste it in or choose the file. It shows what it found before it replaces anything, and Put back what was here before undoes it.

Apple Health

On iPhone and iPad, the app can add each workout you finish to Apple Health: the kind of workout, when, and how long. It asks after your first finished workout. To turn it on or off later, use Save to Apple Health at the foot of the workout screen.

If it says FIT isn't allowed: open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, FIT by Flaux, and turn on Workouts. FIT never reads anything from Health. On Android, the app doesn't save to Health Connect yet.

Training with a mate

Pick 2 people and FIT runs the same timer with a card for each of you, side by side, each with your own moves and reps. To send a workout to someone, tap Share: the link opens the same workout on their phone or in their browser, no app or account needed.

Contact

Anything else, or something not working? Email hello@flaux.ai with your device, whether it's the app or the website, and what you expected to happen.

Check with your doctor before starting if you have an injury or health condition, and stop if anything hurts.
