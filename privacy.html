Privacy

Effective 2 November 2026

The short version

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything FIT knows about your workouts stays on your device. We never see it.

This covers FIT on the web at fit.flaux.com.au and the FIT by Flaux app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

What FIT keeps, and where

FIT saves what it needs on your own device, in your browser's storage or the app's: your settings, the gear you've got, the names you type in, your favourites, the sessions you've done, your program and Surf check results, and how each session felt. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. There is no FIT server that receives it.

Clearing your browser's data, or deleting the app, deletes it. A backup you make (Save a backup, or Copy to another device) is a file or some text that you keep. FIT doesn't upload it anywhere.

Sharing a workout

When you tap Share, FIT packs the workout into a link: its moves and timings, the gear and leave-outs it was built with, and for two people the names on the cards and each named person's level, so it opens the same on the other phone. All of that sits in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to our server. You choose who gets the link.

Apple Health (the iPhone and iPad app)

If you allow it, the app adds each workout you finish to Apple Health: the kind of workout, when it started and when it ended. It asks first, and you can turn it off at any time in the app (Save to Apple Health) or in the Health app.

  • FIT only writes workouts. It reads nothing from Health.
  • What it writes stays in Health on your device (and your own iCloud, if you use it). We never receive it.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

The voice coach and sound

The voice coach uses your device's built-in voices, on the device. Nothing you hear or do is recorded, and FIT never uses the microphone or camera.

The website and the app

The app has everything it needs inside it and doesn't connect to any server of ours. The website is hosted by Vercel, whose servers deliver the pages to your browser and, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs of those requests (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability. We don't add analytics or tracking of our own, and once loaded the website works offline from your browser's copy.

Children

FIT collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
